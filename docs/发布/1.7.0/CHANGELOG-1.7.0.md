# Better Gold 1.7.0 Changelog

**TL;DR:** Two new metal lines - **Chaos Gold** and **Imbalance Alloy** - bring the mod from 8 to **10** metals, together with the **Money Gremlin**, the **Sturdygold Anvil**, **High-Precision Alchemical Oil** and the mod's first **nether structure**, the **Golden Prison**. The handbook grows a whole new category about that prison, the advancement tree goes to **63**, and the Money Gremlin finally has its own voice.

## Highlights

- **Two new metals**: **Chaos Gold** (its own material chain, **11 building blocks** and the full **14-piece gear run**) and **Imbalance Alloy**, the tier above it, forged from Chaos Gold gear plus an Imbalance Alloy upgrade template.
- **Every Chaos Gold piece rolls within a range** instead of using a fixed number: damage, mining speed and damage reduction each have a minimum and a maximum, and the value is re-rolled whenever you **switch items, land a hit, finish mining or take a hit**.
- **Imbalance Alloy** keeps the same idea with **higher ceilings**, and every hit has a **50% chance** to inflict **Strength Imbalance** or **Resistance Imbalance** on the target.
- **The Money Gremlin**, a floating nether mob that hoards coin strings, cuts with the strings around it, fires them in every direction when you run, and makes you hungrier with every coin that lands.
- **The Sturdygold Anvil**: a full anvil that **never accumulates repair penalties** and repairs with **Chaos Coin Strings for 1 level**.
- **High-Precision Alchemical Oil**: a brewing upgrade of Alchemic Fuel that **doubles** the precious-metal raw materials you get.
- **The Golden Prison**, a natively generated nether dungeon with a **unique treasure room**, jigsaw-assembled from 26 templates.
- **A brand-new handbook category** and the **Money Gremlin's own sound set**, all three sounds with **subtitles**.

## New metals

- **Chaos Gold** - the un-noble twin of the precious metals. Ore-free: **Chaos Coins** smelt into **Chaos Gold Nuggets**, nine nuggets make an ingot, and a **Chaos Coin String** breaks down into three coins. Unlike the noble metals it is **not** under the "precious metal" umbrella, but it matches them almost feature for feature: the same **11 building blocks**, the same **14-piece gear run**, and a **randomised** stat line.
  - **Randomised stats**: damage, mining speed and damage reduction all roll **inside a range**; the value changes on **item switch, attack, successful mining and taking damage**. The ranges' upper bounds are deliberately below the noble metals.
  - **Not precious metal, still a substitute**: Chaos Coins and Coin Strings can stand in for part of the **Copper Block, Iron Block, Gold Block and Netherite Ingot** recipes, and Chaos Gold Blocks can substitute in precious-metal raw material recipes - at the cost of needing High-Precision Alchemical Oil.
- **Imbalance Alloy** - Chaos Gold's "absolute disorder" upgrade, made with its own upgrade smithing template. Same randomised stats with **higher caps**, plus a **50% chance on every hit** to apply **Strength Imbalance** (the target's damage is randomised, down to **1**) or **Resistance Imbalance** (the target takes more damage, up to **double**). It triggers from **any** source, including melee swings with a bow or crossbow.
  - **Imbalance Alloy Shield**: ignores shield-breaking attacks, **50% chance** to imbalance the attacker, and it can imbalance while you melee with it.
  - **Imbalance Alloy Armor**: randomised damage reduction plus the same imbalance chance against attackers.
  - **Imbalance Alloy Forbidden Fruit**: the Chaos Forbidden Fruit's stats last **16 minutes** instead of 3, with an equal cooldown.

## New mobs, blocks and items

- **Money Gremlin** - 30 HP, hovers, follows up to 32 blocks. Three attack steps at melee range (4 / 8 / 12), and beyond three blocks it stops and fires **eight coin strings in all directions**. Its coins pass through other Money Gremlins and **always apply a stacking hunger effect, up to 12 levels**. It takes extra damage from **fire, lava and Flamegold weapons**, and drops **Chaos Coin Strings**. Spawns in the Golden Prison, in spawner rooms and in the wild.
- **Sturdygold Anvil** - a full anvil GUI. Combines exactly like a vanilla anvil but **never accumulates the repair penalty**, and repairs with **Chaos Coin Strings at a flat 1 level**.
- **Chaos Coin** and **Chaos Coin String** - the currency line. Strings break down into three coins; coins are throwable, apply stacking hunger up to 12 levels, and can substitute into metal and metal-block recipes.
- **Golden Torchflower** - grown from **Golden Torchflower Seeds** on **Gold-Infused Farmland**. Brew **High-Precision Alchemical Oil** from it on a brewing stand, on a base of Alchemic Fuel.
- **High-Precision Alchemical Oil** - doubles the precious-metal raw material yield; the resulting recipes are otherwise the same as the Alchemic Fuel versions.
- **Gift Gold Ticket** - now also sold by the **Gold Trader's novice tier** for **24 Golden Eggs**, 10 XP each.

## The Golden Prison

- A natively generated **nether structure**, built from **26 jigsaw templates**: an entrance, corridors, small / medium / large cell runs in several styles, two kinds of four-way junctions, and **exactly one treasure room per structure**.
- **Placement matches the vanilla nether fortress** - same spacing and separation, so it turns up at the same kind of density - and it **never generates in Basalt Deltas**.
- Every structure contains **at least three four-way junction pieces**, and the exits are only ever attached to rooms, never bolted onto the end of a corridor.
- The treasure room is stuffed with gold, including **Ancient Debris**, and guarded by more Money Gremlins than anywhere else.
- Structures vary from run to run: cell variants, junction variants and room counts all roll, and ten different seeds give ten different layouts.

## The handbook

- **A whole new category, "The Golden Prison"**, with **6 chapters and 37 pages**: the dungeon itself, the Money Gremlin, Chaos Coins and the Sturdygold Anvil, Chaos Gold's main content, its secondary content and Imbalance Alloy.
- **A new chapter, "Advanced Precious-Metal Extraction"**, explaining how Golden Torchflower and High-Precision Alchemical Oil double your raw material output.
- **A custom page type** that shows **two recipes of different kinds on a single page** (a smelting recipe above, a crafting recipe below) - used by the Chaos Gold chapter.
- The retired Chaos Gold chapters are gone; the handbook now holds **5 categories, 27 entries and 231 pages**.

## Advancements

- **63 advancements**, up from 59.
- The Golden Prison line runs **Red Room Behind Gold** -> **Dancing With Gold** -> **Fine Goldsmithing** / **Bestow the Curse of Disorder**, then branches into the Chaos Gold and Imbalance Alloy gear chains.
- **Fine Goldsmithing only fires on the real substitutions** - the four metal-block recipes and the eight Chaos-Gold-Block raw material recipes. Making a raw material with High-Precision Oil alone no longer awards it.
- The old "obtain a Chaos Coin" and "obtain an Imbalance Alloy upgrade template" advancements were retired.
- The alchemy line gained **Golden Flame!** and **Oily to Refined**.

## Sounds

- The Money Gremlin has its own **rustle, hurt and death sounds**, and all three show up in the **subtitles** in both Chinese and English.
- Being a floating mob, it no longer plays **footstep sounds**.

## Balance and recipes

- **Chaos Gold armor**: helmet 200 / chestplate 264 / **leggings 246** / **boots 220** durability, with **2 armor toughness** per piece and **5% knockback resistance** on each piece and on the shield. The shield has **426** durability.
- **Chaos Gold trident throw** deals 9-13 damage.
- **Chaos Gold shield** is crafted from **7 Chaos Gold Ingots** (all but the bottom two corners). The **Chaos Forbidden Fruit** is 8 ingots around an apple.
- **Chaos Gold mace, trident, crossbow and shield recipes** were reworked to the author's layouts.
- **Thornsgold armor** now takes **25% less sweet-berry-bush damage per piece**, a full set is completely immune, and **sweet berry damage no longer wears the armor down at all**.
- **Echogold armor** cuts **warden sonic-boom knockback by 25% per piece**, a full set cannot be knocked back by a sonic boom at all.
- **Indigoseagold ranged weapons** ignore liquid drag - arrows and bolts fired from Indigoseagold bows and crossbows keep their speed underwater.
- **Money Cowrie** can now be made from the **Golden Eggplant** as well as from a Gold Ingot, both through the Cowrie Mold.
- **The creative inventory** was reordered in five places at the author's request.

## Fixed

- **Silk Touch / handbook text no longer shows `Format error:`** - a bare `%` in a few handbook pages was being read as a format specifier.
- **The Chaos Gold chapter's nugget and ingot recipes now share one page**, instead of being pushed apart onto two.
- **Our own status effects are no longer removed by milk.** Milk still clears every vanilla and third-party effect, and the Totem of Undying still clears ours.
- **Echo Roar no longer hurts its own caster.**
- **The Money Cowrie recipe in JEI showed a Gold Ingot where it should show a Golden Eggplant** - the client was rebuilding the recipe with the wrong catalyst.
- **The Golden Prison's templates now include their air**, so rooms and corridors are no longer filled with netherrack and doorways are a full 3x3.
- **The Golden Prison's entity and block-entity data is exported**, so the golden toad's Chaos Coin eyes, the Money Gremlin spawners and the chest loot all survive jigsaw assembly.
- **The Golden Prison's exits are attached to rooms**, not stacked onto corridor ends, and unfilled openings dropped from 34 to 36 out of a much larger exit count.

## Notes

- **Farmer's Delight** and **Patchouli** remain **optional**. Without Patchouli the handbook is simply not registered; nothing else changes.
- **Existing worlds are unaffected**: the Golden Prison only appears in newly generated chunks, and the creative tab reordering is cosmetic.
