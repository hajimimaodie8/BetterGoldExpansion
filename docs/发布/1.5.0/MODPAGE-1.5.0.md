## English (append to the mod page)

**New in 1.5.0 - Two new metals**

Indigoseagold and Illusiongold join the mod, each as a complete line: raw ore, ingot, nugget and an upgrade smithing template; a block, bricks, stairs, slab, wall, pillar, door, trapdoor, bars, chain and lantern; sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel and hoe; and a helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. Their pillars work as beacon bases, and their blocks have a temper of their own - Indigoseagold blocks bite endermen, blazes, snow golems and striders and refill an axolotl's air, while Illusiongold blocks hand out Regeneration.

Indigoseagold weapons stack Sediment, a suffocating effect that also slows the target down; its armor resists suffocation and drowning, moves you faster in water, and its tools shrug off the underwater mining penalty and hit the four creatures above twice as hard. Illusiongold weapons have a 16% chance to leave a target without its AI for a second, and its armor answers attackers in kind.

Every metal - Sturdygold included - now has five more weapons: mace, shield, bow, crossbow and trident, thirty in all. Shields ignore the axe shield-disable and answer attackers with their metal's effect; a Sturdygold shield, worn with the full Sturdygold armor set, stocks up to twenty points of absorption. Sturdygold weapons keep their 6144 durability and 30 enchantability, the rest use 4096 and 24.

Both integrations stay optional. With Farmer's Delight you also get the Indigoseagold and Illusiongold knives; with MoreUpgradeTemplate installed, its own golden mace, trident, bow, crossbow and shield become the upgrade bases for the six metal versions, while without it you craft the golden blanks yourself.
