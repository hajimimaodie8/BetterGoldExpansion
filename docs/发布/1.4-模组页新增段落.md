# 模组页新增段落（1.4）

> 用途：追加 / 更新模组主页描述时，直接复制对应语言的一节。英文段落沿用 1.3 页面「短段落、平实陈述」的写法。

---

## English（追加到模组主页）

**New in 1.4.0 — Three new metals**

Flamegold, Voodoogold and Thundergold join SturdyGold, each as a complete line: raw ore, ingot, nugget and an upgrade smithing template; a block, bricks, stairs, slab, wall, pillar, door, trapdoor, bars, chain and lantern; sword, axe, pickaxe, shovel and hoe; and a helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. All three sets share 4096 durability, netherite-level mining and 24 enchantability, and a single piece of armor is enough to make piglins neutral.

Every metal has its own personality. Flamegold smelts whatever its tools harvest and sets you alight if you linger on its blocks; its armor cuts fire damage and throws High Burn back at attackers. Voodoogold banks 80% of the damage you take while its Voodoo is active and pays the whole sum out at once when the effect expires; its blocks poison you and its armor resists magic. Thundergold calls a visual lightning bolt on every hit, striking everything in a 3×3 area for 6 extra damage and Tremble, and its armor shrugs off Weakness and Tremble.

The creative inventory is now a single "Better Gold" page divided by five banners — Materials, Building, Food, Gear and Farmer's Delight — so the whole mod is browsable in one place.

Both integrations are entirely optional: with Farmer's Delight installed you also get the Farmer's Delight section, four metal knives and their smithing recipes; with Immersive Engineering installed, a Metal Press recipe turns 4 Golden Cowries into a Golden Cowrie Mold.

## 中文对照

**1.4.0 新增内容 —— 三套新金属**

烈燃金、巫毒金与结雷金加入万坚金的行列，每套都是完整的一条线：原料、锭、粒与升级锻造模板；金属块、砖块、楼梯、台阶、墙、柱、门、活板门、栏杆、链与灯笼；剑、斧、镐、锹与锄；头盔、胸甲、护腿与靴子。三套共用 4096 耐久、下界合金级挖掘与 24 附魔能力，盔甲穿上一件就足以让猪灵中立。

三套金属各有脾气。烈燃金会把它挖到的东西直接熔炼，你在它的方块上待久了会着火；它的盔甲减免燃烧伤害，并把高燃还给攻击者。巫毒金会在巫毒生效期间存下你受到伤害的 80%，等效果自然结束时一次性还给你；它的方块会让你中毒，盔甲抗魔法。结雷金每次命中都会召来一道视觉闪电，对 3×3 范围内的目标造成 6 点额外伤害并施加颤栗；它的盔甲则免疫虚弱与颤栗。

创造模式内容现在合并成一个「更有用的金」页面，用五条横幅分成材料、建筑、食物、装备与乐事五个分区，整个模组一页看完。

两个联动都是完全可选的：装上农夫乐事会多出乐事分区、四把金属小刀与对应的锻造配方；装上沉浸工程则多一条冲压机配方 —— 4 个金钱贝压成一个金钱贝模具。
