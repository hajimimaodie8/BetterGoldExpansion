## English (append to the mod page)

**New in 1.6.0 - Two new metals, a handbook, and 51 advancements**

Thornsgold and Echogold bring the mod to eight metals. Thornsgold weapons and tools infect what they hit with Parasite, a cactus-rot that ticks damage every second and pays a little of it back to whoever applied it; its blocks sting anything that touches them, and its armor shrugs off cactus damage and answers attackers in kind. Echogold weapons and tools land a warden's Sonic Roar - a per-second burst that catches everything in a three-block cube around the target - while its blocks hit back with sonic damage and a shove away from the block. Both arrive complete: ore, ingot, nugget, upgrade template, all eleven building blocks, the full fourteen-piece gear run, four armor pieces and a Farmer's Delight knife. Both pillars work under a beacon.

The mod also has a book now. The Alchemy Student's Handbook is crafted from a book and a gold ingot, and with Patchouli installed it opens a proper in-game guide: four categories, twenty entries and 192 pages covering the alchemy chain, every metal's equipment and its full fourteen-piece upgrade run, golden cooking, and the trader and antiques. Patchouli stays optional - without it the book simply is not there, and nothing else changes.

Fifty-one advancements join it, shaped around the alchemy theme, and all of them are visible from the start. Almost all of them now ask you to *obtain* something rather than craft it, so picking it up, trading for it or getting it as a drop all count - and the Farmer's Delight knives count as that metal's gear. Nine of them are challenges, and the eight metal lines hold a fixed top-to-bottom order instead of being reshuffled on every launch.

Alongside that: an Alchemy Materials Box from the trader's expert tier for seven Gift Gold Tickets, a Golden Rose Bush that dyes down into yellow and can now be planted straight onto Gold-Infused Dirt, a Glittering Vine that drops from leaves and vines with any metal tool, Buried Treasure in the treasure box rotation, and sixteen new config keys that make the config file the single source of truth for every metal's on-hit and armor chances - with config-screen labels that finally name the shield on the weapon side and the four armor slots on the armor side.

The creative inventory's Materials tab now opens with the twenty-one slots in the order you asked for, and netherite antique tools no longer turn Netherite Blocks or noble-gold building blocks into dust on the spot - the dust still drops, so nothing you were relying on is gone.

There is a solid batch of fixes as well. Voodoo armor's full-set poison immunity had never actually worked; the sonic boom sound now plays for creative-mode players and for anything standing inside its own invulnerability frames; Echogold's blocks really do knock targets away the way the book always said they did; Golden Bone Meal now turns up on the hit, not just on the kill; Indigoseagold's trim palette is back to your original artwork; and two advancements that could never fire - "All Set, Smelt It, Taken!" and the planting half of "Tilling My Golden Land" - now do.
