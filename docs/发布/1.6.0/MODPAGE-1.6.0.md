## English (append to the mod page)

**New in 1.6.0 - Two new metals, a handbook, and 51 advancements**

Thornsgold and Echogold bring the mod to eight metals. Thornsgold weapons and tools infect what they hit with Parasite, a cactus-rot that ticks damage every second and pays a little of it back to whoever applied it; its blocks sting anything that touches them, and its armor shrugs off cactus damage and answers attackers in kind. Echogold weapons and tools land a warden's Sonic Roar - a per-second burst that catches everything in a three-block cube around the target - while its blocks hit back with sonic damage and a shove away from the block. Both arrive complete: ore, ingot, nugget, upgrade template, all eleven building blocks, the full ten-piece gear run, four armor pieces and a Farmer's Delight knife. Both pillars work under a beacon.

The mod also has a book now. The Alchemy Student's Handbook is crafted from a book and a gold ingot, and with Patchouli installed it opens a proper in-game guide: four categories, seventeen entries and 148 pages covering the alchemy chain, every metal's equipment and its full fourteen-piece upgrade run, golden cooking, and the trader and antiques. Patchouli stays optional - without it the book simply is not there, and nothing else changes.

Fifty-one advancements join it, shaped around the alchemy theme, and all of them are visible from the start. Most of them now ask you to *obtain* something rather than craft it, so picking it up, trading for it or getting it as a drop all count. The Sturdygold line carries the one challenge.

Alongside that: an Alchemy Materials Box from the trader's expert tier for seven Gift Gold Tickets, a Golden Rose Bush that dyes down into yellow, a Glittering Vine that drops from leaves and vines with a special metal tool, Buried Treasure in the treasure box rotation, and sixteen new config keys that make the config file the single source of truth for every metal's on-hit and armor chances.

There is a solid batch of fixes as well. Voodoo armor's full-set poison immunity had never actually worked; the sonic boom sound now plays for creative-mode players and for anything standing inside its own invulnerability frames; Echogold's blocks really do knock targets away the way the book always said they did; and Golden Bone Meal now turns up on the hit, not just on the kill.
